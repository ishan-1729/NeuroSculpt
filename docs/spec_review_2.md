> Record of the adversarial review of this spec (2026-11-03) and the follow-up consistency check. Four lenses (citations, design, math, feasibility) each had a skeptic try to refute every finding; 57 of 58 survived, many with corrected severity or fixes. Accepted fixes are logged as rows 6–17 in [research_decisions.md](research_decisions.md). Section numbers refer to the spec as it stood when reviewed; citation facts here were checked by automated agents only.
